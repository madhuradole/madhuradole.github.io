###### [Music Genre Classification](https://github.com/madhuradole/Music-Genre-Classification-Machine-Learning-) (Machine Learning – Python, Scikit-Learn, Matplotlib)          Jan. - May 2017 

- Trained 70% of GTZAN Music Genre dataset to build a Genre Classification model. 
- Selected three different feature extraction techniques: MFCC, RMS Energy, and Chroma Vector and trained data on following learning algorithms: SVM, Logistic Regression, Ada-Boost, KNN, Perceptron & Decision Trees. 
- Compared and analyzed the performance of learning algorithms, with SVM proving to be better amongst all, along with MFCC feature extraction technique.
